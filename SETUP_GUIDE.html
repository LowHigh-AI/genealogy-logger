Genealogy Logger

Quick-Start Installation & Setup Guide for Anyone

Step 1

Add Extension to Google Chrome (30 seconds)

  1. Unzip the Genealogy-Logger.zip folder onto your computer (e.g. into Documents or Desktop).
  2. Open Google Chrome and type chrome://extensions in the address bar, then press Enter.
  3. In the top right corner, switch on the "Developer mode" toggle.
  4. In the top left corner, click "Load unpacked".
  5. Select the unzipped Genealogy-Logger folder and click Select / Open.
🎉 That's it! The Genealogy Logger settings tab will automatically open in Chrome.
Step 2

Connect Your Google Sheet (2 minutes)

  1. Create a new spreadsheet at sheets.new (or open any existing sheet in your Google Drive).
  2. In the top menu of Google Sheets, click Extensions > Apps Script.
  3. Delete any sample code in the editor.
  4. Open the google-sheets-script/Code.gs file inside the extension folder (or click "Copy Script Code" on the extension settings page) and paste it into the script editor.
  5. Click the blue Deploy button > select New deployment.
  6. Click the gear icon next to "Select type" > select Web app:
    • Execute as: Me (your email)
    • Who has access: Anyone
  7. Click Deploy, authorize Google when asked, and copy the Web app URL.
  8. Paste this URL into Step 2 of the extension's Settings page.
💡 Note on Google's Authorization screen: Because this is your own personal script, Google may show a standard safety prompt: click "Advanced" and then "Go to Untitled project (unsafe)" to approve your own sheet script.
Step 3

Get Your Free Google AI API Key (60 seconds)

  1. Go to Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com/app/apikey).
  2. Sign in with your Google account.
  3. Click the blue button "Create API key".
  4. Copy your new key (starts with AIzaSy...).
  5. Paste it into Step 1 of the extension's Settings page and click Save Settings!
Usage

How to Log Records While Browsing

Whenever you are viewing an ancestor's record on any website, you can log it in 3 convenient ways:

⌨️ Omnibox "log"

Click your browser address bar, type log + Space, type any optional notes, and press Enter.

🖱️ 1-Click Toolbar

Click the 📜 extension icon in your Chrome toolbar to capture and log the page instantly.

⚡ Keyboard Shortcut

Press ⌘+Shift+L on Mac or Ctrl+Shift+L on Windows.

📜 Right-Click Menu

Right-click any record image, text, or page background and click "Log Genealogy Record".
